Your partnership reaches women around the world.

Because of you:

Women are gathering.

Through our Tea Parties, we're creating spaces where ministry women connect, pray, encourage one another, and find strength in community. As your support grows, we're expanding to more cities across North America, Europe, and beyond.

Leaders are being equipped.

Our annual Retreat brings women together for a transformational 25 to 30 person experience of spiritual renewal, leadership development, and deep community. Women leave renewed, clarified in their calling, and empowered to lead with wisdom and strength.

Financial and leadership development is accessible.

Our Business, Finance & Leadership Summit is equipping women with tools for financial empowerment, entrepreneurial thinking, and sustainable leadership, currently reaching women globally through our virtual platform, completely free.

Resources are reaching women everywhere.

Through our books (Dear Pastor's Wife, Silicon Valley Girl, The Supernatural Woman, and Wisdom for the Workplace) and the articles and content we're creating, women in ministry have access to biblical wisdom and practical tools they can use immediately.

Mentorship and support are creating lasting change.

Through one-on-one coaching and spiritual mentorship, we're walking alongside women in ministry, helping them navigate the unique demands of their calling.

We couldn't build this movement without you. We love and appreciate your partnership.
