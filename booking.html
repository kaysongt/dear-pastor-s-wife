About May on stage

She doesn't stay on the surface.

May is a sought-after speaker at women's conferences and ministry gatherings, and those who have heard her will tell you: she doesn't stay on the surface. With disarming honesty, she makes room for women to face the fears and insecurities they've carried in silence, then challenges them to move. Not just to feel something, but to do something: to confront what's held them back and step into who God has called them to be. Women leave not just inspired, but changed.
