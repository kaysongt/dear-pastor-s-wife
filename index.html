The bigger vision

Not just a site. A living library of help, healing, and leadership support.

Dear Pastor's Wife is growing into a resource destination for women who love God's work but also need language, support, and practical tools for the weight they carry.

The site keeps the soft, modern look — but expands the mission: articles, devotionals, teaching notes, book reflections, event pathways, prayer support, financial empowerment, and community for women in every season of ministry life.

FAQs

Common questions

What is Dear Pastor's Wife becoming?

A resource hub, community, and event pathway for pastors' wives and women in ministry who need spiritual encouragement, practical tools, rest, and connection.

Who can use these resources?

Pastors' wives, ministry wives, women in church leadership, and women supporting ministry communities are welcome.

Where does the book fit?

The Dear Pastor's Wife memoir can anchor reading circles, reflection guides, teaching series, and resources around calling, resilience, family, and authenticity.
