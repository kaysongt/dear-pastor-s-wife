FAQs

Common questions

Are the resources really free?

Yes. The resource library is free and open to everyone. The heart of DPW is to serve first.

Who can join?

Any woman in church leadership or ministry life is welcome, anywhere in the world.

Is my donation secure?

Absolutely. Payments are handled by a PCI-compliant, encrypted provider. We never store your card or bank details.

What do monthly partners receive?

Vision updates, behind-the-scenes photos and video, early invitations, and clear benefits at every tier.

How do I register for an event?

Find your event on the Events page and follow the link, or join the newsletter to be notified the moment registration opens.

Can Pastor May speak at my event?

Yes. Fill out the booking request form on the Bookings page with your event details and the team will follow up.
