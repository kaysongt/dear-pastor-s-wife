About Us

Dear Pastor's Wife — and the woman behind it.

Dear Pastor's Wife began as a letter and grew into a community. What started as honest words for women carrying the weight of ministry has become a global hub of free resources, events, and friendship — built for the women who pour out so much and are rarely poured into.

Founder May Ijisesan knows that world first-hand. For years she served quietly while learning, often the hard way, that thriving in ministry isn't about doing more — it's about being rooted, supported, and reminded of who you are. DPW is the space she wished she'd had.

Pastor May's full bio is on the way and will live here — her ministry journey, her family, and the heart behind Dear Pastor's Wife.

— May Ijisesan

FAQs

Common questions

Are the resources really free?

Yes. The resource library is free and open to everyone — the heart of DPW is to serve first.

Who can join?

Any woman in church leadership or ministry life is welcome, anywhere in the world.

Is my donation secure?

Absolutely. Payments are handled by a PCI-compliant, encrypted provider. We never store your card or bank details.

What do monthly partners receive?

Vision updates, behind-the-scenes photos and video, early invitations, and defined benefits at each tier.

How do I register for an event?

Find your event under the Events tab and follow the link, or join the newsletter to be notified the moment registration opens.

Can Pastor May speak at my event?

Yes — fill out the booking request form under Bookings with your event details and the team will follow up.
